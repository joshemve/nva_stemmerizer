## What we collect

**Nothing automatically, by default.**

The Stemmerizer plugin processes all audio entirely on your local computer.
It does not upload your audio, your project files, or your stems anywhere.
The AI models that perform stem separation are bundled in the installer and
run on your CPU.

The plugin does NOT include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, ad
networks, fingerprinting, or any third-party SDK that exfiltrates data. If
you've configured an offline firewall, Stemmerizer will function fully
offline.

## What we collect when you choose to interact with us

If you contact our support email or website, the personal data you submit
(name, email, message contents, system info you choose to share) will be
processed solely to respond to your request and is retained for **TWELVE
(12) MONTHS** after the last interaction. We do not sell or share this data
with third parties for marketing.

If you purchase the Software through our store, our payment processor
(currently **PAYMENT_PROCESSOR — e.g. Stripe / Gumroad / Paddle**) handles
your payment information. Their privacy policy applies to that processing
and you should review it independently. We receive only your email address,
order ID, country of purchase (for tax purposes), and license key from the
processor.

## License-key validation

Stemmerizer's license key is validated **OFFLINE** by default. If you
enable the optional "online verification" feature in Settings (off by
default), the plugin will contact **LICENSE_SERVER_DOMAIN** once at
activation and then no more frequently than once every 30 days, sending
only your license key, plugin version, and a hashed machine identifier.
This data is used solely to enforce the per-seat license limit set in the
EULA and is retained while your license is active plus 13 months.

## Update checks

If you enable "Check for updates" in Settings (off by default), the plugin
will fetch a small JSON manifest from **UPDATE_DOMAIN** at startup, sending
only the plugin version. No personal data is collected.

## Children

Stemmerizer is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent
minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal
data from children.
